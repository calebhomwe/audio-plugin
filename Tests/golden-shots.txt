Wave 6a, Part C: golden screenshots of MixAgent's editor.

EditorProbe renders the editor at three PARAMETER states - every parameter at its
default, at its minimum, at its maximum - and prints an FNV-1a 64 hash of the
rendered ARGB pixels for each on every run ("golden pixel hash ..."). The editor is
a fixed 1160x920, so the parameter value is its axis of variation: sixty-one
readouts whose width changes with their value, in boxes 52 px wide. A readout that
fits at its default and is condensed to illegibility at its maximum is invisible if
you only ever look at the defaults.

With AGM_SHOT_DIR set it also writes, per state, the PNG and a JSON dump of the
component tree: id, class, bounds, parent, visibility, font size, text, colour and
required width of every string, every meter's scale, and every graphic colour pair
with its composited contrast.

The pixel hash is taken on the PIXELS, not on the PNG file, so it does not move
with zlib's compression level. The PNG sha256 is recorded too, because that is what
a reviewer can check with sha256sum without building anything.

WHAT THESE NUMBERS ARE AND ARE NOT
A change detector, not a portable gate, and printed rather than asserted. Glyph
rasterisation depends on the installed fonts, on fontconfig, on FreeType's version
and on JUCE's. A hard assertion would fail on another machine for reasons that have
nothing to do with the editor. Compare them on the same environment, recorded below.
